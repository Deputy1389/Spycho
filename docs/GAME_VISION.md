# Spycho principles

1. Silence is gameplay and a resource.
2. Information is scarce and imperfect. Sound suggests a place, never a precise enemy indicator.
3. Walls block vision but often not bullets; heavy structure remains protection.
4. Every action can reveal information, including movement, reloads and doors.
5. Shooting is a commitment: scarce ammunition and an enormous break in the silence.
6. Players deceive one another by manipulating information.
7. The map is intimate. Opponents should be meters apart behind opaque partitions.
8. The other armed human, rather than a monster, creates the tension.

Protect **listen → infer → manipulate → commit**. No music, radar, outlines, hit markers, classes, loot, progression, oversized maps or weapon inventory. Prioritize the sound-and-wall-shot interaction over additional systems.
